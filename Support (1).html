Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What types of files can I upload to generate flashcards?

SmartDeck currently supports uploading PDF documents, image files, audio recordings, and raw text. Our AI will automatically analyze these files to extract key information and generate your study decks.

How do I cancel my premium subscription?

Since all subscriptions are handled securely through Apple, you must cancel directly through your device settings:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Tap your name at the top of the screen.
  • Tap Subscriptions.
  • Select SmartDeck: Quiz & Flashcards and tap Cancel Subscription.

How do I delete my account and data?

You have full control over your data. To permanently delete your account, uploaded files, and generated flashcards:

  • Open the SmartDeck app and navigate to your Profile.
  • Tap the Settings icon.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.

Please note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
